Every exam is checked against your date of birth using the latest notification's general-category age rule. Exams sharing a syllabus are grouped into five prep tracks, because you can realistically prepare for one or two tracks at a time.

A

Mechanical JE track

Main track

Your degree is your edge here, and the topic map covers it. RRB JE, SSC JE, ALP, Kerala PSC AE, ISRO, ESE.

B

Railway & SSC general

Pair with A

Maths, reasoning and GA. Already needed for RRB JE CBT-1 and SSC JE Paper 1, so NTPC and CGL cost little extra.

C

Banking & insurance

Only if you want a bank career

English, quant, reasoning. Several last chances fall in 2027, but the syllabus is separate from A.

D

Civil services & police

Needs full-time commitment

UPSC CSE, KAS, Kerala SI. Three CSE attempts left; a year or more of dedicated prep each.

E

Kerala PSC general

Long-term backup

Degree-level prelims and other Kerala posts. Open until about 2034, so there is no rush.

Where the numbers come from

  • RRB JE: actual question counts averaged over three 2024–25 CBT-2 shifts (Make It Easy analysis). Theory of Machines and Machine Design got no questions in those shifts, though older papers asked about 12.
  • SSC JE: coaching estimates for Paper 2 (100 questions) from recent papers, using the midpoint of each range (Physics Wallah). Engineering Mechanics shows up mainly in Paper 1 (8–10 Qs).
  • Kerala PSC: official marks per module from the AE (Mechanical) syllabus, 100 marks total. Diploma-level posts like Overseer use different modules.
  • Sub-topic tiers are based on recurring PYQ patterns, not exact counts, since no reliable sub-topic tallies are published.
